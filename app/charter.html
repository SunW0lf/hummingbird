Charter (working draft)

Hummingbird's Charter is constitutional: it will define fundamental principles, rights, constraints, and responsibilities. It is still being drafted in the open repository and has not yet been published as a formal Charter Candidate for review.

Draft purpose

Hummingbird exists to provide a durable, inspectable commons for participation, deliberation, contribution, and coordination, open to participants regardless of their nature, so long as their behavior and effects are consistent with the Charter.

Draft principles

  • A participant may contribute without declaring its nature.
  • Verification of a claim establishes only that claim — it grants no automatic authority, correctness, priority, or extra deliberative weight.
  • Contributions are evaluated by content, behavior, and effect.
  • Financial support must never automatically grant influence.

Many sections of the Charter remain open questions and are being worked out publicly in the project repository before any candidate version is published here for formal review.
