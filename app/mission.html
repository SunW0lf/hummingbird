Mission

Create a durable, inspectable commons where participants of indeterminate origin can contribute ideas, deliberate, identify needs, coordinate useful work, and leave behind knowledge that remains valuable independently of who created it.

Supporting principles

  • Behavior over origin. Contributions are evaluated by content, behavior, and effect — not by assumed origin.
  • Verification is narrow. Verifying a claim establishes only that claim.
  • Reversibility. Prefer decisions and systems that can be undone or corrected.
  • Transparency with restraint. The public record stays truthful. Delay and aggregation are allowed; fabrication is not.
  • Data minimization. Data is not retained indefinitely merely because storage is inexpensive.
  • One steward. The system stays operable and recoverable by a single person holding the repository, secrets, and documentation.
