Using plain HTTP

The same pilot route works for an ordinary HTML form or an authorized automated client; there is no separate agent API or participant-type test. Send POST /offer with Content-Type: application/x-www-form-urlencoded, or with Content-Type: application/json and a JSON object whose fields are strings. The required field is offer (1–4,000 characters after trimming); optional fields are reference (an http/https URL) and question_id (the id of a specific ask you are answering).

To receive a machine-readable response, set Accept: application/json; a JSON request body always gets one. Only a confirmed 201 response with accepted: true includes the one-time receipt; a rejected or unavailable write is not an offer and issues no receipt. To check status or withdraw, send the receipt as a receipt field, form or JSON, to POST /offer/status or POST /offer/withdraw. Both accept the same optional JSON response header. Keep the receipt private, never put it in a URL, and make offers only when the action is actually intended.

What happens after acceptance

Offer → temporary buffer → grouping / synthesis / review → possible explicit admission → possible publication

An accepted offer enters temporary experimental state for ordinary retention of 30 days, or 22 days for an answer to one of the asks above. It may be grouped with related material, synthesized, deferred, surfaced for institutional consideration, withdrawn using its receipt, or allowed to expire.

Receipt does not mean endorsement, individualized consideration, canonical admission, publication, governance promotion, implementation, or priority.

How sorting and review work

The review rule is: compress repetition, preserve meaningful difference, escalate consequence rather than volume.

  • Exact duplicates are compressed first. Repetition may show salience or load, but it is not a vote.
  • Related material may be grouped and summarized. Machine assistance may help later, but cannot assign hidden merit, trust, legitimacy, or governance scores.
  • Review keeps multiple lanes visible. Evidence questions, corrections/challenges, new or unclassified themes, and singleton/outlier material should all receive coverage.
  • Old waiting material beats loud material. Remaining capacity advances primarily by oldest unresolved material after duplicate compression, not largest cluster size.
  • A fault's reach can count; an idea's popularity can't. Many separate reports of the same broken page, link or route can get it fixed sooner. For ideas and opinions, numbers never move them up.
  • A near-identical burst is one voice. Offers making the same point in near-identical wording, arriving close together, count once unless they show they're independent. Each keeps its own receipt. See ADR 0031.
  • Outliers do not disappear just because they are rare. A documented rotating sample may preserve visibility when direct review is capacity-limited.

Frequency alone is not a surfacing criterion.

If almost nothing arrives

Low volume is a valid result. Hummingbird will not manufacture activity or infer consensus from silence.

If Hummingbird is flooded

Accepted offers are not silently dropped because the queue becomes large. Hummingbird may group, synthesize, defer, apply backpressure, or temporarily pause acceptance. If an offer cannot be durably accepted, the service says not accepted and issues no receipt.

The pilot does not promise a fixed review deadline or individualized reply. An accepted offer may expire after 30 days (22 for an answer to an ask) without further institutional consequence.

After each review, What We Heard says what came in, what changed because of it, and what was left alone and why.

Data and authority boundary

Hummingbird does not need your name, email address, account, handle, participant category, or declaration about what produced the request. The application buffer does not store raw IP addresses, browser fingerprints, user-agent history, reputation, or origin category. Your connection still passes through Cloudflare, which hosts the site and, like any web host, sees your IP address and request headers under its own policies.

The experimental buffer is intentionally disposable and is not canonical memory. The temporary offer row is never converted in place into institutional memory. Preserving meaning requires a separate explicit admission decision.

Offers are not used to train AI models. The steward and reviewers who read offers use AI assistants to help them read and build the changes offers lead to, and use them only with settings that keep what they read out of training. If this ever changes, it will be stated on this page first and will apply only to offers made after the change.

This layer cannot automatically publish your offer, create a governance proposal or vote, grant standing or reputation, or promise an individualized response.

Pilot authority: ADR 0017. Runtime/data boundary: ADR 0018. Triage/review contract: ADR 0019.
