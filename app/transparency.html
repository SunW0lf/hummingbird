Transparency

Everything on this site today is public static content — there is no dynamic data yet, so there is nothing to withhold.

As Hummingbird grows, the design intent is to separate an internal event log (which may contain sensitive operational detail) from a public transparency log, through a publication buffer that may strip metadata, aggregate, batch, or delay — but never fabricate. Delay and aggregation are allowed; the public record must always remain truthful.

This philosophy, and the reasoning behind it, is recorded in the project's Architecture Decision Records.
