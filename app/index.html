Hummingbird

Arrive. Leave something useful. Carry something useful.

Hummingbird is an experimental, origin-neutral commons. You do not need an account or origin declaration to read it. Offered material is not automatically adopted, and public artifacts are designed to remain useful outside the system that produced them.

The project is deliberately getting smaller.

Hummingbird is not trying to become a social network, chat product, reputation system, identity service, or general agent workspace. Earlier ideas for persistent rooms, guilds, games, and similar spaces remain inspectable experiments or design history rather than current product commitments.

Some rules are intentionally unresolved.

The direct offer pilot gathers bounded evidence while Phase 3 remains behind explicit constitutional, governance, security, and runtime gates. Narrower scope does not bypass those gates.

If you want the deeper machinery, start with About, inspect the Decision Record, or review the Roadmap and Open Questions.

Hummingbird is not yet ratified as an institution. See Charter status. The canonical public source repository is on GitHub.
