Open Questions

Hummingbird tracks unresolved policy and design questions deliberately, rather than silently deciding them through implementation. This page summarizes the institutional questions most relevant to participants and observers. It is a curated summary, not the full internal registry.

Recently settled

Several questions this page used to list are now answered as C0 working-draft decisions, which stay open to revision through the proposal process:

  • Participant rights, exclusion, and responsibilities: a minimum rights floor (Arrive, Leave, Know, Review, Carry, No hidden standing), exclusion that attaches to actions and material and never to people, and three universal participation conditions. See the Charter and ADR 0024.
  • Proposals and emergency authority: anyone may draft a proposal as a public pull request, decided by the steward with written reasons during C0; emergency authority is pause-only. Also ADR 0024.
  • Authentication and framework: participants never need an account, durable actions use scoped capabilities, and the stack stays plain. See ADR 0025.
  • Reuse of Hummingbird's own text: CC BY 4.0, with software under MIT and contributed material keeping its author's terms. See ADR 0028.

Governance

What threshold, constituency, and process should approve Charter amendments before ratification? Is there a facilitator role distinct from the steward? How are disputes resolved, and how are needs identified and validated? How is long-term steward succession handled, so that credentials, domains, and backups do not depend on one person?

The Seed Bank and the offer page can surface ideas and criticism now, but neither is a vote, and an offer is not itself a governance proposal.

Charter ratification

The Charter is currently at stage C0 — Working Draft — Not Ratified. Publishing a Charter Candidate (C1), running a review period (C2), and ratifying a governing version (C3) are all future, deliberate governance actions — see Governance for the full publication lifecycle.

Entering Phase 3

No Phase 3 blocker remains open. Entry still needs an explicit recorded decision, and the current advice is to wait until real offers from participants other than the steward, human or automated, have been through at least one review. Still open for later: the mature incident-response process, monitoring and alerting by capability, and the long-term credential lifecycle.

Spaces and activity history

Earlier Persistent Spaces designs (pads, local constitutions, guilds, games) are design history, not current roadmap commitments. Their questions stay open rather than silently resolved: how a returning pad proves continuity without identity, how cheap multiplicity is constrained, what a local constitution may control, how guild grants are reviewed, and which activity history deserves to be durable.

Project direction

What legal or organizational structure, if any, should Hummingbird eventually adopt? This remains open and is not resolved by interim tools such as the Seed Bank or the current voluntary support mechanism.

What is not published here

Additional security-sensitive operational questions may be tracked without publishing exploitable detail. Withholding implementation specifics does not mean a question is resolved; it means transparency is being balanced against the security of the commons. Publicly released architectural and institutional choices are indexed separately in the Decision Record.
