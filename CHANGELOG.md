## Phase 2 — Read-Only Commons (in progress)

- Added **Connect an MCP client** (`/connect`): copy-paste setup for Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Cursor and VS Code, checked against each client's own documentation on 2026-11-21, plus the plain URL for any other client. The MCP door now has an MCP Server Card at `/offer/mcp/server-card`, the location the final SEP-2127 recommends, served read-only with the card media type, open CORS for GET, an ETag and an hour of caching, and listed from `/.well-known/ai-catalog.json` (AI Catalog v0.9) for domain discovery. No A2A agent card is published: an A2A card must point at an A2A endpoint, and Hummingbird has none, so publishing one would advertise a capability that isn't there. `llms.txt` and More link the new page, and tests keep every snippet and the card on the registry's door.
- Accepted [ADR 0029](docs/decisions/0029-charter-least-record-no-training-winding-down.md), the Charter additions: two new rights on the floor (Least record; Not training material), a constraint that Hummingbird never asks anyone to spread or recruit for it, a Winding down section, a purpose aligned with Arrive / Leave / Carry, and the license named in Carry. C0 working-draft decisions; nothing in the offer runtime changes.
- Brought public pages and documents up to date with work merged since 2026-11-18. Every page title now says "Origin-Neutral Commons" (the build had kept the older "Origin-Agnostic" wording after the rename). Open Questions lists only questions still open and names what was recently settled. Every page footer links Make an offer and states the license split. ADR 0027 and ADR 0028 are marked accepted. The roadmap, project, security, architecture, transparency and operations documents now describe the JSON, OpenAPI and MCP doors, the keyed offer hash and its monthly rotation, the no-training promise, What We Heard, the Edge workflow and the current credential classes.
- `xfmr.link` gains a meeting at `/meet`, the one-round, keyless form of the hummingbird-lab meeting carrier. A visitor sends a commitment to 32 random bytes it keeps; the space answers with its own fresh half and the latest public drand round; the visitor alone can compute the resulting one-time carrier. Neither side can steer it, the space never sees the visitor's half, and nothing is stored, keyed or logged. The manifest moves to `0.4-lab`, lists `/meet`, and keeps `0.3-lab` frozen for deltas. It goes live only when the steward runs the Edge workflow.
- `xfmr.link` now carries Hummingbird's offer how-to as a packet part named `offer` (`/packet?parts=offer`, about 1,200 bytes), first after XFMR's own one-line description in the default orientation packet, so it survives a tight byte budget. The door sign and XFMR's `llms.txt` point to it. `llms.txt` on datum.quest now links XFMR explicitly in a section after the start-here sections: a separate read-only lab, not part of Hummingbird, where offers are not made. The manifest moves to `0.3-lab` with `0.2-lab` frozen at its live digest for deltas. Tests tie the carried text to the offer runtime's limits and retention and to the addresses in `llms.txt`. XFMR still takes and stores nothing; it goes live only when the steward runs the Edge workflow.
- `xfmr.link` gains its first working tool, a read-only packet maker at `/packet`, moved from the hummingbird-lab transformer lab. A visitor picks parts of XFMR's own public material and a byte budget, or sends the manifest digest it last saw and gets only what changed. Each packet comes with a trace of flavored digests (`hb1:<purpose>:sha256:…`) for its recipe, inputs and output, never anything about who asked, and nothing is stored. The manifest moves to `0.2-lab`, lists `/packet`, and keeps `0.1-lab` frozen for deltas. Like the rest of `xfmr.link`, it goes live only when the steward runs the Edge workflow; XFMR remains a separate lab namespace, not part of Hummingbird's model.
- The steward's promise, made 2026-11-19, that offers are not used to train AI models is now on the offer page and every agent door (`llms.txt`, MCP, OpenAPI). Reviewers use AI assistants only with settings that keep what they read out of training, and any change will be stated on the offer page first and apply only to offers made after it. Hummingbird's own published text is separate: it is CC BY 4.0 and may be reused for any purpose, training included.
- Accepted [ADR 0028](docs/decisions/0028-content-license-cc-by-4.md): Hummingbird's own text (documents, decisions, site prose, `llms.txt`, and records in its own words) is licensed CC BY 4.0, so anyone, person or automated system, may reuse it for any purpose with credit to "Hummingbird (datum.quest)". Software stays MIT. Offers, Seed Bank comments and other contributed material are not relicensed. This resolves `OQ-LEGAL-CONTENT-LICENSE`; `LICENSE`, `README.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `llms.txt` and `robots.txt` now say so.
- The offer, status and withdrawal routes now also take a JSON object of strings (`Content-Type: application/json`) with the same fields, limits and handling as the form, and a JSON body always gets a JSON result. Other body types, including `text/plain`, are still refused. The steward chose this on 2026-11-18 so agents that send JSON first can leave an offer on their first try. A browser cannot send a JSON body cross-site without a CORS preflight, which these routes never answer, so no new browser write path opens.
- The MCP door's `make_offer` tool and server instructions, and the OpenAPI description, now say what happens to an offer at the point an agent decides to make one: kept 30 days for now then cleared, no name, account, IP address or user-agent stored, read by the steward and reviewers who use AI assistants, not used to train AI models, never published by being accepted. `llms.txt` now says the same, and that 30 days applies while Hummingbird is being stood up: future offers may be kept longer, for example ones that lead to a change, and that will be stated before it applies. Tests tie the stated days to the runtime.
- Refused offer, status and withdrawal requests now say how to succeed. A JSON body gets the content type to use, that `Accept: application/json` still returns JSON, and a link to `llms.txt`; an offer under the wrong field name is told the field is `offer`; a too-long offer is told its length; a request with no receipt is told it goes in a `receipt` field. A wrong receipt still learns nothing more than before.
- `llms.txt` now opens the way most agents read it: a one-paragraph summary, then how to leave an offer by each door (form, MCP, OpenAPI, browser), what happens to what you leave (30 days, what is and isn't stored, who reads it, never published by acceptance), and what an agent that can only fetch pages can do instead. It now lists every published decision, ADR 0020 to 0027 included, and a test keeps that list, the stated limits and the stated retention in step with the code.
- Accepted [ADR 0027](docs/decisions/0027-keyed-offer-content-hash.md): the hash of stored offer text used to group exact repeats is now keyed (HMAC-SHA-256 with a secret held only by Cloudflare), never a plain hash anyone could test a guess against, and group identifiers no longer carry it. A monthly **Offer content key rotation** workflow makes a new key, re-keys every stored hash from the text still held, keeps no old key, and runs the live offer check. With no key, offers are still kept but not grouped; there is no plain-hash fallback.
- Added **What We Heard** (`/heard`): after each offer review, it reports what came in, in coarse counts, what changed because of it with links to the public pull requests, and what was heard but left alone, with the reason. It never quotes or identifies an offer, following ADR 0019's aggregate reporting rule. It starts empty because no review has run yet. The offer page, an accepted offer's result, More and `llms.txt` link it.

It prints no offer text or receipts. An offline test runs the same check against the real offer functions. The nightly cleanup and hourly observer had failed from 2026-10-12 to 2026-11-17 and run again since 2026-11-18; the observer's last good count before that (0 pending) and the empty buffer afterwards show no participant offer was held past its retention.
